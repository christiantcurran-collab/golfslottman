St. George, Utah

3 Nights • Red Rock Golf • Desert Paradise

Why St. George?

Utah's Golf Paradise

St. George offers stunning red rock desert golf with incredible scenery and excellent value. Located in southwestern Utah, it enjoys over 300 days of sunshine annually. Less crowded than Scottsdale with equally dramatic landscapes and championship courses carved through red rock canyons.

65-80°F Average Red Rock Views Great Value
