Scottsdale, Arizona

3 Nights • 4 Championship Courses • Desert Golf Paradise

Why Scottsdale?

The Desert Golf Capital

Peak season for desert golf with near-guaranteed sunshine (avg highs 72-77°F). Stunning mountain backdrops, excellent dining scene in Old Town Scottsdale, and world-class courses that offer a truly unique golfing experience.
